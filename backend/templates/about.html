1. Project Objective 🎯
ExoHabitAI predicts the habitability potential of exoplanets using planetary and stellar parameters obtained from the NASA Exoplanet Archive dataset. The system applies supervised Machine Learning techniques to classify exoplanets into habitable or non-habitable categories based on astrophysical constraints.
2. Scientific Basis of Habitability 🌍
Planetary habitability depends on critical astrophysical factors:

• Presence of liquid water • Location within the stellar Habitable Zone • Moderate equilibrium temperature • Balanced stellar radiation exposure • Stable planetary mass and radius

Planets too close to their host star may overheat, while distant planets may freeze. ExoHabitAI evaluates these conditions computationally.
3. Dataset Source 📊
The dataset was obtained from the NASA Exoplanet Archive, including confirmed exoplanets discovered by missions such as Kepler and TESS, along with detailed planetary and stellar parameters.

5. Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering 🔧
Missing values were removed to ensure reliability. Outliers with extreme physical parameters were filtered. Derived features such as planetary radius and insolation flux were calculated to improve predictive performance. A binary habitability label was created using astrophysical thresholds.
6. Machine Learning Preparation 🤖
Relevant features were selected to model temperature stability and radiation exposure. The dataset was split into 80% training and 20% testing data. Proper validation ensured unbiased model performance evaluation.
