Famous Exoplanets

The most promising worlds discovered beyond our solar system.

Kepler-442b

1,206 LY Away Habitable Candidate

A super-Earth exoplanet orbiting within the habitable zone of a K-type main-sequence star. It is one of the most promising candidates for potential habitability.

Proxima Centauri b

4.2 LY Away Closest Exoplanet

The closest known exoplanet to the Solar System. It orbits in the habitable zone of the red dwarf star Proxima Centauri.

TRAPPIST-1e

40 LY Away Earth-Sized

One of seven Earth-sized planets in the TRAPPIST-1 system. It is considered one of the most potentially habitable exoplanets found so far.

Kepler-186f

580 LY Away Earth Cousin

The first Earth-size planet discovered in the potentially habitable zone. It orbits a red dwarf star.

Kepler-22b

600 LY Away Water World?

A planet in the habitable zone of a Sun-like star. It might be covered in a super-ocean.

LHS 1140 b

40 LY Away Super-Earth

A dense rocky planet in the habitable zone of a red dwarf, offering excellent conditions for atmospheric study.
