What Are Exoplanets?

Click cards to reveal details.

🌍

Definition

Click to Learn

An exoplanet is any planet beyond our solar system. Most orbit other stars, but free-floating exoplanets, called rogue planets, orbit the galactic center.

🔭

Discovery

Click to Learn

Scientists use various methods: Transit (dimming starlight), Radial Velocity (star wobble), Direct Imaging, and Microlensing.

🌡️

Habitable Zone

Click to Learn

The "Goldilocks Zone" is the region around a star where conditions are just right for liquid water to exist on a planet's surface.

🚀

Missions

Click to Learn

NASA's Kepler and TESS missions have discovered thousands of exoplanets by staring at patches of sky and measuring brightness.
